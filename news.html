Oct 2026 · Workshop

AI Literacy Workshop

The workshop opened with an introductory lecture on physical AI, covering foundational components such as models, motors, and sensors. Participants then experienced the full AI workflow hands-on: collecting image data, labeling road signs, and training their own recognition model. Finally, they flashed the model onto a smart car and watched it detect signs and turn in live movement on the playfield — seeing their data drive real hardware.

Proven in the Classroom

Our AIoT ecosystem served as the core curriculum and final-project material for a leading UG course in HK, with the previous generation of smart cars and playfields tested by over 100 students — proving a superior interactive learning experience. We are now extending the platform to secondary school STEAM education.
